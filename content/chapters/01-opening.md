The corpus opens the way most works do: with an arrival. Someone
comes to a place they have not been, and the place is not finished
with them.

This chapter carries `order` and `dek`, the two fields the literary
templates use. In chapbook the dek renders beneath the chapter title
and in the table of contents; in pamphlet it is inert front matter
and the chapter reads the same without it.

## What follows

A second chapter with no dek, an unnumbered chapter with no `order`
at all, and a draft. Between them they cover every row of the
portable field table for `content/chapters/`.
