The corpus's first post carries the fields the blogs use: a `date`,
a `tag`, and a title. In prose-blog and tech-blog it joins the posts
collection, the feed, and the tag pages. In the literary templates
the date and the tag are inert front matter and the post builds as a
plain page.

The date also lives in the filename, the way the blogs' own demo
posts do; the two agree.
